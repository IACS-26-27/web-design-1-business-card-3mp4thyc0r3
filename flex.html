My layout uses flex model strategies to organize and design a secondary business card for the same studio as the first. The front of the card employs the use of "justify-self: center" and flexbox movements. On the back of the card, the two text blocks use "flex-direction: column;" and padding to space themselves along the card, and the image of our title game "The Little Lamp Girl" uses absolute positioning to be set apart from the text.
